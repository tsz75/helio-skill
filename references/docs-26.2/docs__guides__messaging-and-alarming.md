
# Messaging & Alarming
Every machine needs to provide messages to ensure proper functioning and
prevent damage or safety risks. Therefore HELIO has built-in support for
triggering messages, listing active messages and finally displaying an archive
of past messages.
Messaging Settings and
Messages View give you all the tools you need to send
error, warning, and information messages to your operators. Follow this
guide to send clear, consistent, and helpful messages to your operators
that make a real difference.
