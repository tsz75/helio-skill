
# Download HELIO
One of HELIO's primary objectives is to provide an engineering tool that
eliminates hardware constraints. That's why you can install HELIO as a
standalone solution or as a dedicated edition for app-based ecosystems.
To ensure that HELIO runs smoothly…
…just make sure your hardware, your operating
system, and the browser that renders your HMI meet the
Minimum System Requirements.

### HELIO Standalone
Allows you to run HELIO on your development PC
with any of the following operating systems: **Windows**, **Linux**, and **macOS**.
Of course, HELIO Standalone also runs on your target devices such as a
PLC or an
IPC. HELIO Standalone has been successfully deployed on industrial
hardware by **Beckhoff**, **B&R**, **KEB**, and **Siemens**.

### HELIO Editions
HELIO is available as an app for the following ecosystems:
- ctrlX OS by Bosch Rexroth
- Next Open Automation (NOA) by KEB
- HELIO Standalone
- HELIO ctrlX OS Edition
- HELIO NOA Edition
