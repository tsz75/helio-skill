
# Licensing Help
Flexibility is a guiding principle in both our HMI development process
and our licensing model. HELIO is available as `Developer License` and
`Runtime License` in different sizes.
These sizes are categorized based on factors such as the number of data
sources, connected clients and the maximum number of connected HMI variables
in your projects. This way you only pay for what you need and can scale your
licenses as needed.
You can find a list of all licenses on the HELIO Website:
→ https://helio-hmi.com/en/pricing
