
# Access Control

## Goals
Industrial machines are used by individuals with a variety of skill sets and
educational backgrounds. That's why, as HMI Engineers, we want to:
Lock a machine's operation
for unauthorized users.
Adapt HMIs to the abilities and understanding
of different user roles.
Boost operator confidence
by reducing complexity.
Empower experts
to tackle a wide range of complex tasks.
