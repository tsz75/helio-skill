
# Guiding Operators
Providing guidance and handlebars to operators is especially crucial in HMIs
due to the following reasons:
- **Operators possess different skill levels**. While experienced operators who
have been using a machine for years may not need much guidance, new and
inexperienced team members will benefit from this support to make informed
decisions.
- **Industrial Machines are quite complex creatures**, comprised of numerous
components that must function in unison, even though they are assembled by
different suppliers. One of the main responsibilities of the HMI is to
seamlessly integrate these parts, creating a unified product.
The guides in this series aim to assist operators through simplicity, while
minimizing exposure to complexity.
