
# Styleguide for Writing & Content
»The words you choose within your app are an essential part of its user experience.«
https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/writing
This styleguide ensures a consistent and joyful HELIO documentation as well
as consistent UX writing for HELIO HMIs and IDE.
The `Styleguide for Writing & Content` is not an official part of the documentation.
It will not be published to the official docs URL.
However, it is available during local development or within specialized deployments.
